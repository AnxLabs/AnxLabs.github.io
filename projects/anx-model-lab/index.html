ANX Model Lab

Exploring native AI models, from the first weights to evaluation.

ANX / MODEL+

The project

Start at the model itself.

ANX Model Lab investigates model development from randomly initialized weights, with work around training, tokenization, datasets, evaluation, and checkpoints.

It is a research project for exploring architecture and training choices. Production usefulness and independently reproduced performance are not established.

What it explores

  • Training and architecture experiments
  • Tokenization and dataset workflows
  • Evaluation and checkpoints

Where it stands

Research stage. No production-ready model or public model distribution is claimed.

In the ecosystem

Native models could eventually support parts of the ecosystem. The documented HQ integration is explicitly design-only.
